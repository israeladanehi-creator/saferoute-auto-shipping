Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SafeRoute

What types of vehicles can I ship? ▼
We handle all types of vehicles: sedans, SUVs, trucks, commercial vehicles, and specialty cars. Just provide the vehicle details when creating your tracking ID.
How do I track my shipment? ▼
After creating your tracking ID, you'll receive a unique tracking code. Use it on our website, mobile app, or dashboard for real-time updates.
What's included in your pricing? ▼
Our pricing includes vehicle transportation, real-time tracking, insurance coverage, and customer support. No hidden fees.
Do you offer international shipping? ▼
Yes, we specialize in international import/export logistics. We handle port-to-warehouse and cross-border shipments with full customs support.
What if my vehicle is delayed? ▼
We guarantee 99.8% on-time delivery. If there's any delay, our team notifies you immediately with a revised timeline and compensation if applicable.
Is my shipment insured? ▼
All shipments include comprehensive insurance coverage against damage, theft, and loss. Full value protection is standard with every tracking ID.
